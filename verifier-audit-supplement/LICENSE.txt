Terms of use

The first-party text, scripts, schemas, and derived count tables in this
archive accompany the paper so that readers can check its counts. Anyone may
run, copy, and inspect them for that purpose. This permission doesn't cover
third-party material, and it doesn't grant permission to redistribute the
archive.

THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt describes the source datasets and software. The
archive contains no benchmark task text and no evaluator code.
